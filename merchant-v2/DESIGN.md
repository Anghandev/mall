# Merchant Workbench Design Direction

The operator uses this interface on a bright shop or warehouse workstation
during active fulfillment. The UI is a light, dense work surface with warm
paper neutrals, ink text, controlled gold for primary focus and green only for
verified states.

- Typography: native sans stack; tabular numerals for amounts and identifiers.
- Layout: 248px desktop navigation, compact top bar and full-width work areas.
- Shape: 6px controls and panels; no nested cards or decorative pills.
- Color: restrained warm neutrals with gold, green, amber and red semantic roles.
- Motion: no decorative motion; interaction state remains immediate and stable.
- Responsive: sidebar becomes a labeled drawer; tables scroll within stable
  regions; forms collapse to one column under 760px.
