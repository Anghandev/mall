# 24K商城 · 商家工作台

- Register: product
- Users: one-product merchants, store operators and fulfillment staff.
- Purpose: operate a single product, fulfill delivery and pickup orders, inspect
  segregated assets, and review immutable activity without calculating Core
  financial or finality truth in the browser.
- Tone: precise, restrained and operational. Chinese labels come first.
- Trust rule: unavailable Core projections are visibly unavailable, never
  rendered as zero or replaced by demo success.
- Anti-references: marketing dashboards, decorative crypto neon, card grids,
  local transaction simulation and merchant-side forced receipt.
