## Closure

No frozen business rule above is open for reinterpretation. Missing external
systems are `ExternalBlocked` or `ConfigurationRequired`; missing publication
of an otherwise confirmed wallet list or policy is configuration failure, not
permission to invent a commercial default. Neither status authorizes a fixture,
disabled placeholder or simulated success.
